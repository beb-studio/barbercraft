# PRODUCT: BarberCraft

## The Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform for Barber & Hairdresser Businesses

BarberCraft is an operational business platform. It is NOT primarily a consumer marketplace. The primary audience using the application to manage their day-to-day operations are the business owners, managers, and staff members of grooming businesses.

### Core Audience
1. **Business Owners & Managers**: Need a high-level view of revenue, staff utilization, and daily agenda. They require a professional, trustworthy, and sophisticated operational view.
2. **Staff Members (Barbers, Hairdressers)**: Need a clear, friction-free way to check their daily schedule, time-offs, and upcoming appointments. They need maximum legibility and speed.
3. **Platform Administrators**: Need macro-level oversight across all tenants (businesses) using BarberCraft.
4. **Customers**: End users booking an appointment via the public flow. The booking flow must be seamless, mobile-optimized, and conversion-focused.

### Design Paradigm: Operational SaaS Interface
- **Light-First Only**: The interface is intentionally designed as a premium, light-themed administrative tool. It avoids "barber clichés" like pure black backgrounds and gold gradients. This is a tool for professional businesses.
- **Sophisticated & Trustworthy**: The platform needs to feel stable, secure, and clear.
- **Information Hierarchy & Density**: We optimize for displaying data and schedules clearly. Mobile layout should be compact but legible. Desktop layout must leverage available screen real estate (no arbitrary max-widths that artificially constrain operational views).
- **Language**: Technical documentation and code are in English. Every user-facing UI string must be in Turkish.

### Current Evolution
- Replaced the initial generic, dark AI-generated UI with a custom light-first Design System (Task 011A).
- The product relies strictly on its multi-tenant foundation: Platform Admins → Business Owners → Staff Members.
- Tenant-Scoped Customer CRM & Operations: First-class customer directory, appointment history, internal notes, existing customer selection in manual bookings, duplicate review, and tenant-safe logical customer families. OWNER/ADMIN can explicitly choose a primary customer and confirm a logical merge; the product never auto-merges. Canonical CRM reads, alias-aware search, stale-ID redirects, retained lineage, and future-booking resolution operate on the canonical family while historical appointment and note foreign keys remain untouched. V1 intentionally provides no unmerge operation (Tasks 013A through 013C.2C).
- Accountless Public Appointment Management: every successful public booking receives a one-time-delivered, 256-bit capability link. PostgreSQL stores only its SHA-256 hash. The `/randevu#<token>` convention keeps the secret out of HTTP request URLs/access logs, removes it from browser history immediately after capture, and presents a private/no-store/noindex appointment summary. A valid capability may deliberately cancel or reschedule a future `PENDING`/`CONFIRMED` appointment after confirmation. Public rescheduling changes date/time only, retains the concrete staff, service, snapshots, status, and customer identity, and revalidates the selected slot against current server truth.
- Business-Controlled Public Scheduling Policy: each tenant owns centralized booking lead-time, optional maximum-advance, cancellation-notice, and reschedule-notice rules. Compatibility defaults preserve existing behavior (`0`, unlimited, `0`, `0`). Public booking and self-service use current trusted business policy; internal staff scheduling is not constrained by these customer-facing rules.
- Operational Booking Policy Controls: tenant OWNER and ADMIN users configure those four rules at `/dashboard/settings/booking` with lossless minute/hour/day inputs, an explicit unlimited horizon, current-policy summaries, and stale-edit protection. Public calendars reflect trusted lead/horizon constraints, while appointment management explains when online cancellation or rescheduling notice windows have closed.
- Durable Notification Event Foundation: appointment creation, date/time rescheduling, and transition to cancellation now commit a provider-neutral, versioned intent in PostgreSQL inside the same transaction. The outbox retains audit/retry state, excludes raw management capabilities and recipient snapshots, and can be safely claimed by concurrent future workers. TASK 016A sends no SMS, email, or WhatsApp message.
- Provider-Neutral Notification Planning: each business has safe-by-default notification settings (master switch off, transactional event preferences retained) and up to three whole-minute reminder offsets. A server-only planner atomically turns claimed outbox events into idempotent pending/cancelled plans, reconciles future operational reminders when settings change, and never stores recipients, raw management capabilities, or provider credentials. TASK 016B still sends no external message.
- Real SMS Delivery Layer & Worker: Turn due pending plans into concrete, idempotent SMS delivery rows (`notification_deliveries`). The appointment and planning domains remain completely provider-independent. Provider calls occur strictly in the delivery layer via a generic `SmsProvider` adapter interface, with Netgsm as the initial Turkish transactional SMS provider. Public management capabilities are issued transiently immediately prior to transmission, ensuring raw tokens and full `/randevu#` URLs are never persisted anywhere in notification databases, provider payloads, or logs. Concurrent workers safely claim batches using `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and 5-minute leases with exponential backoff on retries (TASK 016C).
- Business Analytics & Query Foundation: Tenant-private, read-only analytics domain enabling business owners and managers to monitor daily agenda volumes, completed appointments, cancellations, no-shows, staff workloads, top services, and near-term upcoming appointments. Ranges (`TODAY`, `LAST_7_DAYS`, `LAST_30_DAYS`) use half-open business-local calendar boundaries in the business's authoritative timezone. Crucially, booked service values are derived from immutable `service_price_snapshot` records for `COMPLETED` appointments and labeled 'Tamamlanan Hizmet Tutarı'—distinct from collected revenue since no payment ledger exists. Reuses established CRM canonical family resolution for logical customer counting, excludes cancelled appointments from upcoming operational counts, and aggregates fully in PostgreSQL without N+1 queries (TASK 017A).
- Business Dashboard & Analytics UX: Turns `/dashboard` into a responsive daily command center for `OWNER` and `ADMIN` powered by TASK 017A's authoritative read model. Features a URL-driven segmented range selector (`Bugün`, `Son 7 Gün`, `Son 30 Gün`), real-time operational status strip (today's totals, remaining today, completed today, next appointment card with calm empty state), 6 restrained KPI metric cards with explicit non-revenue labeling ("Tamamlanan Hizmet Tutarı" with explanatory helper text), an accessible lightweight SVG/CSS daily trend chart with tabular view toggle, top 5 services ranking, neutral staff workload distribution, peak weekday/hourly density summaries, and customer acquisition mix. Retains today's appointment agenda stream for immediate daily management. Crucially, `STAFF` users accessing `/dashboard` receive a dedicated operational personal agenda experience (`StaffDashboardView`), strictly preventing privilege escalation or leak of business-wide analytics or financial data (TASK 017B).
