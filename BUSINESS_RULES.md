# Business Rules: Multi-Tenant Barber Appointment Platform

## 1. Dynamic Catalog Rules (Strict Requirement)

1. **No Hardcoded Services**:
   - The application must **never** hardcode service names (e.g. "Haircut", "Beard Trim"), service descriptions, prices, or durations in client or server code.
   - All services must be retrieved dynamically from the `services` table scoped to the specific `business_id`.
2. **Variable Durations**:
   - Each service has an explicit `duration_minutes` property (must be > 0 and <= 1440).
3. **Dynamic Pricing**:
   - Base service pricing is set in the `services` table using exact PostgreSQL `numeric(10,2)` storage (never floating-point).
   - Price must be non-negative (`price >= 0`).
4. **First Service Onboarding**:
   - Only the authenticated business `OWNER` can create the first service during onboarding (STAFF and ADMIN prohibited).
   - First service is created with `active = true` server-side (no toggle exposed in onboarding).
   - Services remain completely dynamic: zero hardcoded barber services, durations, or prices.
   - Successful creation advances onboarding step to `OWNER_STAFF`.

---

## 2. Staff & Calendar Rules

1. **Independent Calendars**:
   - Each staff member maintains their own calendar, weekly shift schedules (`staff_working_hours`), and time-off/break blocks (`time_blocks`).
2. **Shift Boundary Enforcement**:
   - An appointment can only be booked within a staff member's working hours (`start_time` to `end_time` for that day of the week, 1 = Monday ... 7 = Sunday).
   - Open days must have `start_time < end_time`. Closed days must have both times NULL.
3. **No Double Booking**:
   - A staff member cannot have overlapping active appointments.
4. **Time Blocks**:
   - Types: `TIME_OFF`, `BREAK`, `BLOCKED`.
   - `staff_id` NULL represents a business-wide block (e.g. public holiday) and is permitted only for type `BLOCKED`.

---

## 3. Customer Booking Lifecycle (6 Steps)

1. **Step 1: Service Selection**:
   - Customer selects a service dynamically provided by the business.
2. **Step 2: Staff Member Selection**:
   - Customer selects a specific staff member or opts for "Any Available Barber".
3. **Step 3: Date Selection**:
   - Selected date cannot be in the past or on closed days.
4. **Step 4: Available Time Selection**:
   - Available time slots generated by server-side scheduling logic.
5. **Step 5: Contact Information**:
   - Full Name, Phone (normalized to E.164 per business), and optional notes.
   - Unauthenticated customers are stored in `customers` table scoped to `business_id` with `UNIQUE(business_id, normalized_phone)`.
6. **Step 6: Appointment Creation**:
   - Status defaults to `BOOKED`.
   - Appointment captures permanent snapshots (`service_name_snapshot`, `service_duration_snapshot`, `service_price_snapshot`, `service_currency_snapshot`). Future changes to the service catalog will not alter historical appointment records.

## 5. Owner Staff & Onboarding Completion Rules

1. **Separation of Role and Bookable Identity**:
   - `business_members.role = 'OWNER'` is an authorization and ownership concept.
   - `staff_profiles` is an independent calendar entity representing a bookable service provider.
   - An OWNER may or may not have a `staff_profile`.
2. **YES/NO Decision**:
   - **YES (Bookable Barber)**:
     - Atomically creates `staff_profiles` linked to the OWNER's `business_member_id`.
     - Flags: `active = true`, `public_booking_enabled = true`, `uses_business_hours = true`.
     - Requires selecting $\ge 1$ active service belonging to this business; persisted to `staff_services`.
     - Reuses business working hours (`uses_business_hours = true`) without duplicating schedule rows.
   - **NO (Manager Only)**:
     - No `staff_profile`, `staff_services`, or staff hours are created.
     - OWNER manages the business without appearing in customer booking calendars.
3. **Atomic Onboarding Completion**:
   - Either choice executes in a single database transaction updating `businesses.onboarding_completed_at = now()`.
   - `onboarding_completed_at` semantics: `NULL` = onboarding in progress; non-null = onboarding complete (resolves to `COMPLETED` and unlocks `/dashboard`).

## 6. Service Management Rules (Post-Onboarding)

1. **Authorization & RBAC**:
   - `OWNER` and `ADMIN` have full authority to view, create, edit, and activate/deactivate services for their business.
   - `STAFF` members are strictly forbidden from managing services (cannot access `/dashboard/services*`, cannot invoke service mutation actions). Authorization is enforced strictly on the server; client-provided roles, user IDs, or business IDs are never trusted.
2. **Tenant Isolation**:
   - All service operations (listing, creating, updating, toggling active state) are strictly partitioned by `business_id` derived server-side from the authenticated session membership.
   - Cross-tenant service access or modification attempts are blocked at both repository query and action validation layers.
3. **No Physical Deletion (Soft Lifecycle)**:
   - Services must never be physically deleted from the `services` table.
   - Availability is governed solely via the `services.active` boolean flag (`true` = bookable/active, `false` = archived/deactivated).
   - Deactivating a service preserves full database history and referential integrity.
4. **Historical Snapshot Preservation**:
   - Existing appointments reference the service via foreign key (`appointments_service_fkey` with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`) and capture frozen snapshots (`service_name_snapshot`, `service_duration_snapshot`, `service_price_snapshot`).
   - Modifying a service's name, description, duration, or price updates future bookings only. Historical appointment snapshots remain completely immutable and unaffected.

## 7. Staff Management & Self-Hosted Invitation Rules

1. **Self-Hosted Manual Invitation Architecture**:
   - No third-party email SaaS, external providers, or background mailers are used.
   - When an `OWNER` or `ADMIN` creates an invitation, the system generates a secure one-time link (`/staff/setup?token=<raw-token>`) returned strictly once in the UI for manual sharing.
2. **Cryptographic Token Security & Hashing**:
   - Tokens are generated using Node's `crypto.randomBytes(32)` (256-bit entropy).
   - Only the SHA-256 hash is persisted in `staff_invitations.token_hash`. Raw tokens are never stored in the database and never logged.
   - Server-enforced TTL: Invitations expire automatically after 7 days (`expires_at = now() + 7 days`).
3. **STAFF-Only Scope & Role Safety**:
   - Invitations do not include a client-selectable or database-stored role column.
   - Every invitation created is strictly for the `STAFF` role, preventing privilege-escalation or spoofing risks.
4. **Distinct Domain Model Separation**:
   - `Better Auth user`: Authoritative authenticated identity.
   - `business_member`: Business tenancy membership with role (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`, `STAFF`).
   - `staff_profile`: Bookable provider on the appointment calendar.
   - Issuing an invitation creates **zero** fake users, memberships, or staff profiles. These are created atomically upon acceptance in TASK 005B.2.
5. **Duplicate Prevention & Database Uniqueness**:
   - The database enforces at most one `PENDING` invitation per business and normalized email using a partial unique index (`staff_invitations_one_pending_idx`).
   - Existing active members cannot be re-invited. Stale/expired invitations are superseded cleanly.
6. **Staff Deactivation & Reactivation Lifecycle**:
   - `OWNER` and `ADMIN` may deactivate or reactivate `STAFF` members.
   - Deactivation sets `business_members.active = false` and cascades to `staff_profiles.active = false` and `staff_profiles.public_booking_enabled = false`.
   - Reactivation restores active flags without creating duplicate records.
   - `OWNER` and `ADMIN` roles are immutable within staff management and cannot be deactivated.
   - Deactivation never physically deletes users, business members, staff profiles, or historical appointments.

## 8. Staff Invitation Acceptance & Account Setup Rules (TASK 005B.2)

1. **Truthful Two-Stage Authentication & Acceptance**:
   - Stage 1 establishes or verifies user identity via Better Auth (`signIn.email` or `signUp.email`).
   - Stage 2 authoritatively consumes the invitation via `acceptStaffInvitationAction` on the server.
   - Server NEVER assumes authentication succeeded merely because the client auth API returned success. The server checks `requireSession()` and enforces that the normalized session email strictly matches the invitation email.
2. **Authoritative Server Identity & Token Consumption**:
   - User identity (`userId`) is strictly derived from the validated server session, never accepted from client payloads.
   - Token validation searches the database using SHA-256 hash lookup only.
   - Expired, revoked, or already accepted invitations are firmly rejected with user-friendly errors.
3. **Atomic Acceptance & Membership Provisioning**:
   - Executed inside a single PostgreSQL transaction (`executeStaffInvitationAcceptance`):
     1. Atomically transitions `staff_invitations.status` from `PENDING` to `ACCEPTED` with `accepted_at = now()`, conditional on `expires_at > now()`.
     2. Creates or reactivates `business_members` row with `role = STAFF` and `active = true`.
     3. Creates or reactivates `staff_profiles` row with `active = true`, `public_booking_enabled = false`, and `uses_business_hours = true`.
   - Re-submitting an already accepted invitation is idempotent and rejected without side effects.
4. **OWNER Onboarding State Isolation**:
   - STAFF users are NOT treated as "COMPLETED" within the OWNER business onboarding state machine.
   - For STAFF members, `getOnboardingStep()` and `getOnboardingState()` return `"NOT_APPLICABLE"`.
   - Accepting a STAFF invitation NEVER mutates `businesses.onboarding_completed_at`.
   - STAFF users bypass OWNER onboarding entirely and go directly to `/dashboard`. Any attempt by a STAFF user to access `/onboarding/*` routes is redirected to `/dashboard`.
5. **Scope Boundaries**:
   - No staff-service assignment or custom working hours are configured during acceptance.
   - Staff profiles remain `public_booking_enabled = false` until explicitly enabled after service assignment in TASK 005C.

## 9. Staff Service Assignment & Booking Readiness Rules (TASK 005C.1)

1. **Role Hierarchy & Management RBAC**:
   - `OWNER` users can manage service assignments, toggle public booking, and view profile details for any staff profile in their business (including their own).
   - `ADMIN` users can view and manage `STAFF` profiles only.
   - `ADMIN` users are strictly forbidden from viewing or modifying the `OWNER`'s staff profile, role, membership, or booking settings.
   - `STAFF` users are strictly forbidden from managing staff profiles.

2. **Derived Booking Readiness Engine**:
   - Booking readiness is a derived server-side state. No `booking_ready` column is persisted in the database.
   - A staff profile is booking-ready (`isReady = true`) if and only if all 5 conditions are simultaneously satisfied:
     1. Business membership is active (`business_members.active = true`).
     2. Staff profile is active (`staff_profiles.active = true`).
     3. Public booking is enabled (`staff_profiles.public_booking_enabled = true`).
     4. At least one ACTIVE assigned service exists (`activeAssignedServicesCount >= 1`).
     5. A complete schedule source exists (`uses_business_hours = true` AND exactly 7 `business_working_hours` records are configured).
   - If any condition fails, `isReady = false` and descriptive human-readable reasons are returned.

3. **Public Booking Toggle Prerequisites**:
   - Enabling public booking (`public_booking_enabled = true`) is strictly rejected unless all prerequisites (membership active, profile active, >= 1 active assigned service, complete 7-day schedule) are satisfied.
   - Disabling public booking (`public_booking_enabled = false`) is always permitted for authorized users.

4. **Service Assignment Synchronization (`syncStaffServices`)**:
   - All newly assigned services must belong to the caller's business and must be currently active (`active = true`).
   - Duplicate service IDs are automatically de-duplicated.
   - Existing active service mappings are synchronized: deselected active services are deleted from `staff_services`, and newly selected active services are inserted.
   - **Legacy Inactive Service Preservation**: Existing mappings for services that are currently inactive in the business catalog are preserved unless the authorized user explicitly removes them. Inactive mappings never silently disappear when updating active services.
   - Inactive assigned services do NOT count towards active service count or booking readiness.
   - If active assigned services count reaches 0 during synchronization, `public_booking_enabled` is automatically set to `false`.

5. **Service Catalog Deactivation Cascading**:
   - When a service is deactivated via catalog management (`setServiceActive(..., false)`), existing `staff_services` records are preserved (never deleted).
   - In the same operation, any staff profile that is left with 0 remaining active assigned services automatically has `public_booking_enabled` flipped to `false`.

## 10. Custom Staff Working Hours & Schedule Modes (TASK 005C.2)

1. **Schedule Modes**:
   - **Business Hours Mode (`uses_business_hours = true`)**:
     - The staff member inherits the business's weekly hours (`business_working_hours`).
     - Any existing rows in `staff_working_hours` remain stored in the database but are completely ignored for availability and readiness.
     - Mode switch preserves existing custom rows in case the user switches back.
   - **Custom Hours Mode (`uses_business_hours = false`)**:
     - The staff member uses their dedicated schedule in `staff_working_hours`.
     - Saving custom hours automatically sets `staff_profiles.uses_business_hours = false`.

2. **Canonical Working Hours Data Model**:
   - `staff_working_hours` utilizes a single canonical boolean column: `is_working`.
   - **Open / Working Day**: `is_working = true`, `start_time IS NOT NULL`, `end_time IS NOT NULL`, `start_time < end_time`.
   - **Closed Day**: `is_working = false`, `start_time IS NULL`, `end_time IS NULL`.
   - Strictly enforced at database level via check constraint `check_swh_hours`:
     `((is_working = false AND start_time IS NULL AND end_time IS NULL) OR (is_working = true AND start_time IS NOT NULL AND end_time IS NOT NULL AND start_time < end_time))`.
   - Start and end time formats are validated and normalized to canonical `HH:mm:ss`. Closed days always normalize times to `null`.

3. **7-Day Weekly Schedule Completeness**:
   - Custom staff schedule operations require exactly 7 records representing days 0 to 6 (0 = Sunday, 1 = Monday, ..., 6 = Saturday).
   - Missing days, duplicate days, or out-of-range days are strictly rejected.
   - Schedule replacement is atomic: all 7 days are replaced in a single transaction.

4. **Booking Readiness Evaluation Across Modes**:
   - Readiness engine (`evaluateBookingReadiness`) branches purely on `uses_business_hours`:
     - If `uses_business_hours = true`: verifies `business_working_hours` has 7 records AND at least 1 open day (`is_closed = false`).
     - If `uses_business_hours = false`: verifies `staff_working_hours` has 7 records AND at least 1 working day (`is_working = true`).
   - If an effective schedule has 0 open/working days (all 7 days closed) or is incomplete (< 7 days), booking readiness fails with a specific explanatory reason.

5. **Atomic Public Booking Auto-Disable Rule**:
   - If a staff member has `public_booking_enabled = true`:
     - Saving a custom schedule where all 7 days are closed (`is_working = false`) automatically sets `public_booking_enabled = false` in the same database transaction.
     - Switching schedule mode (from custom to business, or business to custom) where the target effective schedule is not booking-ready (e.g., < 7 days or 0 open days) automatically sets `public_booking_enabled = false` in the same database transaction.
   - **Never Auto-Enable**: When custom hours are saved with open days or mode is restored, `public_booking_enabled` is NEVER automatically set to `true`. Enabling public booking remains an explicit action by an authorized manager.

6. **Authorization & Tenant Isolation**:
   - `OWNER` can view, switch mode, and update working hours for any staff member in their business (including their own).
   - `ADMIN` can manage `STAFF` profiles only. Any attempt by an `ADMIN` to view or modify an `OWNER` profile's working hours or schedule mode is rejected with a 403 Forbidden error.
   - `STAFF` users have zero management permissions over working hours or schedule modes.
   - Client action payloads (`saveStaffWorkingHoursAction`, `setStaffScheduleModeAction`) do NOT accept `businessId`. All tenant scopes and caller roles are authoritatively determined from the authenticated session on the server.

## 11. Staff Time Off / Break / Block Management (TASK 006A)

1. **Purpose and Domain Separation**:
   - Time blocks represent staff unavailability intervals (vacation, sick leave, lunch breaks, administrative closures, etc.).
   - Time blocks are **NOT** appointments. They do not have customers, services, pricing, or payment statuses.
   - They serve purely as unavailability inputs for the future availability engine.

2. **Strict Block Types**:
   - Allowed block types are strictly restricted to:
     - `TIME_OFF` (vacation, personal leave, sick leave, off days)
     - `BREAK` (lunch break, short rest periods)
     - `BLOCKED` (staff meetings, training, custom manual unavailability blocks)
   - Enforced by PostgreSQL enum `time_block_type` and TypeScript union type. Legacy types (`VACATION`, `BUSINESS_CLOSURE`, `OTHER`) and arbitrary strings are rejected by both DB and application validation.

3. **Concurrency-Safe Overlap Prevention**:
   - A staff member cannot have overlapping time blocks.
   - Overlap condition: `new.startTime < existing.endTime AND new.endTime > existing.startTime`.
   - Back-to-back blocks are explicitly allowed: `new.startTime = existing.endTime` or `new.endTime = existing.startTime`.
   - Self-overlap during block updates is excluded by comparing `id != currentBlockId`.
   - **Concurrency Serialization**: To eliminate race conditions where two simultaneous requests might pass overlap checks before inserting, all insert and update operations serialize on the staff profile by executing `SELECT id FROM staff_profiles WHERE id = targetStaffId AND business_id = businessId FOR UPDATE` inside the database transaction.

4. **Business Timezone Strategy & DST Handling**:
   - Users submit wall-clock dates and times (`YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:mm`) in the context of the business's configured IANA timezone (`businesses.timezone`, e.g., `Europe/Istanbul`).
   - Timestamps are persisted in the database as UTC instants using PostgreSQL `timestamptz`.
   - Formatted back to the business's wall-clock timezone for UI display.
   - **DST Gap Rejection**: Wall-clock times that do not exist due to daylight saving time spring-forward transitions (e.g. 02:30 during a 02:00 -> 03:00 jump) are detected and rejected with a descriptive error message. Ambiguous times during fall-back transitions deterministically map to the earlier instant.

5. **Past Block Immutability & Status Lifecycle**:
   - Blocks are dynamically categorized based on authoritative server/DB current time:
     - `ACTIVE`: `startTime <= now() AND endTime > now()` (currently in progress)
     - `UPCOMING`: `startTime > now()` (future block)
     - `PAST`: `endTime <= now()` (completed block)
   - **Audit Preservation**: Past blocks are strictly read-only. Any attempt to update or delete a block whose `endTime <= now()` is rejected.
   - Active and upcoming blocks can be updated or deleted by authorized users.

6. **Working Hours & Booking Readiness Independence**:
   - Time blocks are completely decoupled from weekly working hours:
     - Blocks may occur on closed days, during off-hours, across multiple days, or inside normal shift hours.
     - Creating, updating, or deleting a time block **never** mutates `public_booking_enabled`, staff active status, or booking readiness evaluation.
   - Booking readiness remains solely a function of: (1) active staff status, (2) >= 1 active assigned service, and (3) a complete 7-day schedule with >= 1 open day.

7. **Authorization & RBAC Rules**:
   - `OWNER` can view, create, edit, and delete time blocks for any staff member in the business (including their own profile).
   - `ADMIN` can manage time blocks for `STAFF` profiles only. Any attempt by an `ADMIN` to manage blocks for an `OWNER` profile is rejected with 403 Forbidden.
   - `STAFF` users cannot manage time blocks.
   - Multi-tenant isolation: Client payloads never accept `businessId`. The target business ID is derived from the authenticated caller's session, and cross-business block operations are rejected with 404/Forbidden.

## 12. Public Booking Foundation Rules (TASK 007A)

1. **Public Route Structure & Anonymous Customer Access**:
   - Canonical entry route: `/book/[slug]`.
   - Access is completely unauthenticated and anonymous; no Better Auth session or customer account is required.
   - Public booking routes are excluded from dashboard auth guards and navigation chrome.
2. **Business Public Eligibility Rule**:
   - A business is publicly bookable if and only if:
     1. It exists by exact `slug` match.
     2. `businesses.active = true`.
     3. `businesses.onboarding_completed_at IS NOT NULL`.
   - Incomplete or deactivated businesses return safe not-found / unavailable states without leaking internal onboarding state details.
3. **Public DTO & Data Exposure Boundary**:
   - Server strictly serializes public DTOs (`PublicBusiness`, `PublicService`, `PublicStaff`).
   - Internal identifiers, email addresses, Better Auth user IDs, business_member IDs, roles, internal notes, private phone numbers, and raw Drizzle records are strictly forbidden from being passed to client components.
4. **Active Service Filtering**:
   - Only `services.active = true` records belonging to the business are displayed.
   - Prices preserve exact database numeric strings.
5. **Staff Service-Level Eligibility**:
   - A staff member is selectable for a service if and only if all 8 conditions are met:
     1. `business_members.active = true`
     2. `staff_profiles.active = true`
     3. `staff_profiles.public_booking_enabled = true`
     4. `services.active = true`
     5. `staff_services` mapping exists for this staff member and service
     6. Service belongs to the same business
     7. Staff profile belongs to the same business
     8. Structurally valid effective schedule:
        - If `uses_business_hours = true`: `business_working_hours` has 7 days AND >= 1 open day (`is_closed = false`).
        - If `uses_business_hours = false`: `staff_working_hours` has 7 days AND >= 1 working day (`is_working = true`).
6. **Date-Specific Availability Deferred**:
   - Staff eligibility at this step is service-level only. Date-specific slot calculation, calendar checks, existing appointments, and time blocks are deferred to the schedule step (TASK 007B).
7. **Tenant Isolation & Tampering Protection**:
   - All server actions and schedule validation steps re-verify tenant boundaries and eligibility server-side.
   - Non-UUID or cross-tenant parameters are rejected safely without exposing internal database errors.
   - Schedule progression URL: `/book/[slug]/schedule?service=<serviceId>&staff=<staffId>`.

## 13. Public Booking Schedule Rules (TASK 007B)

1. **Schedule Route & Eligibility Prerequisites**:
   - Canonical schedule route: `/book/[slug]/schedule?service=<serviceId>&staff=<staffId>`.
   - Requires valid `service` and `staff` UUID query parameters.
   - Server re-verifies public business eligibility (`active = true`, `onboarding_completed_at IS NOT NULL`), service eligibility, and staff service-level eligibility (the 8 conditions from Section 12).
   - Invalid or mismatched parameters safely redirect back to `/book/[slug]` without exposing stack traces or database errors.
2. **Authoritative Server Time Boundary (`referenceNow`)**:
   - Public server actions (`getPublicAvailabilityAction`) and client payloads MUST NEVER accept `referenceNow`.
   - Authoritative server time (`new Date()`) is always enforced across the public trust boundary.
   - Internal domain services (`getPublicAvailability`, `validatePublicSlotSelection`) support optional `referenceNow?: Date` strictly for deterministic unit and regression testing.
3. **Business Timezone Calendar Calculation**:
   - Calendar date selection operates strictly in the business's authoritative timezone (`businesses.timezone`).
   - `getBusinessLocalToday(timeZone)` evaluates the current local date in the business's timezone, correctly handling midnight boundary transitions.
   - Public customers may select any date on or after business-local today without arbitrary upper limits (e.g. no 7-day or 14-day hard cap).
   - Past dates relative to business-local today are rejected by server actions and disabled in the UI.
4. **Availability Engine Consumption & Read-Only Semantics**:
   - Public slot generation delegates directly to the hardened `getStaffAvailability` core availability engine.
   - Evaluates business booking interval (`businesses.booking_interval_minutes`), service duration, effective working hours (business or custom staff hours), staff time blocks, and existing appointments.
   - Strictly read-only: NO slot holds, NO temporary reservation locks, NO Redis keys, NO unconfirmed appointments are created.
   - Slots are represented by exact start instants (`startAt` in UTC ISO format) alongside local formatted times (`formattedStartTime` - `formattedEndTime`).
5. **Client UI State & Stale Request Protection**:
   - Changing the selected date immediately clears any previously selected slot and enters loading state.
   - Asynchronous slot queries carry an incrementing monotonic request counter to prevent race conditions or stale slot overwrites when dates are switched rapidly.
   - Distinct empty states are rendered for:
     - Staff not working on this day (scheduled day off).
     - Staff working but all slots are fully booked or blocked.
6. **Confirmation Step Progression & Live Slot Revalidation**:
   - Schedule step progresses to `/book/[slug]/confirm?service=<serviceId>&staff=<staffId>&start=<UTC_ISO>`.
   - The URL carries only `start` (UTC ISO string); service end time is derived authoritatively on the server.
   - The confirmation placeholder derives the business-local date from `start` and the business's timezone, re-invoking `getStaffAvailability` to revalidate the slot in real time before rendering details.
   - If the selected slot is no longer available or invalid, the customer is shown a friendly alert and a link to return to the schedule picker.

## 14. Customer CRM & Operational Selection Rules (TASK 013C.1)

1. **Existing Customer Selection in Manual Appointments**:
   - Authorized operators (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`, and `STAFF` for their own calendar) can select an existing tenant customer when creating a manual appointment.
   - Operators can seamlessly toggle between "Mevcut Müşteri" (search and pick) and "Yeni Müşteri" (manual name/phone entry) modes.
2. **Profile Mutation Safeguards**:
   - Selecting an existing customer during manual appointment creation **MUST NOT** mutate the customer's CRM profile (`first_name`, `last_name`, `phone`, or existing notes).
   - The appointment transaction links directly to `existingCustomerId` within the verified business tenant, bypassing `upsertCustomer`.
   - Appointment-specific notes are persisted directly to `appointments.notes` and do not alter customer-level internal notes.
3. **Purpose-Limited Customer Search by Role**:
   - `searchCustomersForBookingAction`: Permitted for `OWNER`, `ADMIN`, and `STAFF`. Returns minimal operational fields (`id`, `firstName`, `lastName`, `phone`, `appointmentCount`, `lastAppointmentDate`).
   - Customer CRM directory (`/dashboard/customers`), customer details (`/dashboard/customers/[customerId]`), profile editing, and internal notes remain strictly restricted to `OWNER` and `ADMIN`.
   - `STAFF` members are forbidden from accessing the customer directory, profile editing, and internal notes.
4. **Advisory Duplicate Candidate Detection**:
   - Duplicate detection groups customer records by canonical E.164 phone comparison keys (`customerPhoneComparisonKey`).
   - Detection is strictly **advisory and read-only**.
   - Accessible only at `/dashboard/customers/duplicates` by `OWNER` and `ADMIN` (403 for `STAFF`).
   - The system NEVER performs automatic customer merging or automatic deletion.
5. **Customer Merge Architecture Deferred**:
   - Merge UI and canonical-aware read resolution are deferred to TASK 013C.2B/013C.2C.
   - Database trigger `prevent_appointment_column_change()` strictly forbids changing `customer_id` on existing appointments.
   - TASK 013C.2A provides the explicit logical-merge domain foundation without changing historical records.

## 15. Logical Customer Merge Foundation Rules (TASK 013C.2A)

1. A canonical customer has no merge target; an alias points directly to one canonical customer in the same business.
2. Only active tenant `OWNER` and `ADMIN` memberships may execute a logical merge. Platform role alone grants no tenant authority.
3. V1 merge eligibility requires the same non-empty persisted `phone_comparison_key`; name equality never authorizes merge.
4. The complete current comparison-key group is reloaded and locked in deterministic UUID order inside one PostgreSQL transaction.
5. Existing valid alias families are flattened to the newly selected canonical root. Self-merge, cycles, and multi-hop lineage are forbidden.
6. Merge never updates `appointments.customer_id`, reassigns `customer_notes.customer_id`, or deletes customer rows.
7. Direct profile mutation of an alias is rejected with a typed domain outcome; canonical-aware editing is deferred.
8. `normalized_phone` remains the operational identity field. `phone_comparison_key` is non-unique and serves duplicate eligibility and future alias resolution only.
